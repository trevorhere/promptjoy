# PromptJoy Specifications

White-labeled AI enablement platform for enterprises.

## Product Overview

PromptJoy provides companies with their own AI enablement hub at `{company}.promptjoy.co`, featuring:

- AI readiness surveys + analytics
- Conversational learning system
- Claude-powered Q&A with company knowledge
- Team dashboards + progress tracking
- Enterprise integrations (Teams, Outlook, etc.)
