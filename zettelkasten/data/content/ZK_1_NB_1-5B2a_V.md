# ZK I Zettel 1,5B2a

## 1,5B2a of every individual, made a community, they 
 have thereby made that community one body, with a 
 power to act as one body, which is only by the will 
 and determination of the majority. For that which acts 
 any community being only the consent of the 
 individuals of it, and it being necessary to that which 
 is one body to move one way, it is necessary the body 
 should move that way whither the greater force 
 carries it, which is the consent of the majority. …" 

 The unity of the state follows from the unanimous 
 social contract; the unity of state action is 
 secured through the thereby agreed upon, because rational, majority 
 principle.
