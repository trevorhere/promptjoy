# ZK I Zettel 1,6f1A

## 1,6f1A [[1-6f1|1]] corresponds to the idea of unity of leadership 
 as opposed to unity of command; cf. [[42-2|42,2]]. 

 The problem of the unity of the coherence 
 of decisions (provisional, inadequate title) has been discussed particularly in 
 American administrative theory. 
 Cf. e.g. [[Bibliography/simon_smithburg_thompson_1950_administration|Simon - Smithburg - Thompson, S. 513ff.]] Analysis of the 
 various sources of values that influence administrative decisions 
 leads to insight into the contradictoriness and 
 imbalance of administrative action. Cf. in summary 
 [[Bibliography/simon_smithburg_thompson_1950_administration|S. 540]]: 

 "Further, as a result of the particularistic structure of 
 interest groups, and the corresponding structure of 
 legislative bodies and the administrative branch, the 
 pattern of administration is full of inconsistencies. [[Continuation of text on slip 1-6f1A1|]]
