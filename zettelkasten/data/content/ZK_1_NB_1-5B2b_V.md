# ZK I Zettel 1,5B2b

## 1,5B2b Unlike with [Hobbes](/bestand/register/personenregister/item/hobbes_thomas) , reason here does not demand 
 a maximally effective state as such, but only a state that has the powers 
 (legal form: trust) that it needs to effectively protect the property of its 
 citizens. Cf. [[Bibliography/locke_1946_treatise|§ 131]] : 

 "But though men when they enter into society give up 
 the equality, liberty, and executive power they had in 
 the state of nature into the hands of the society, to be so 
 far disposed of by the legislative as the good of the 
 society shall require; yet it being only with an 
 intention in every one the better to preserve himself, 
 his liberty, and his property (for no rational creature 
 can be supposed to extend change his condition with an 
 intention to be worse), the power of the society, or [[Fortsetzung des Textes auf Zettel 1-5B2c|]]
