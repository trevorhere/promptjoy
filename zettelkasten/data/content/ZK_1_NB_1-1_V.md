# ZK I Zettel 1,1

## 1,1 Unity can be organized 

 Contrast: mystical establishment of unity, 
 conscious and methodical (!) non-action. 
 e.g.: in Quaker sects all unification of will occurs 
 through silent prayer, until a 
 member is illuminated and speaks the will of God 
 for the congregation. 

 cf. [[Bibliography/scheler_1926_soziologie|Scheler, Die Wissensformen und die Gesellschaft, S. 63f.]] 

 Organization consciously leaves room open for 
 the influx of what cannot be organized. 
 Limit! 

 cf. AIII 24h [*]
